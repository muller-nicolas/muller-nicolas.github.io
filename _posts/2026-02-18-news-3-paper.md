I am happy to announce that our paper _Magnetic reversals in a geodynamo model with a stably–stratified layer_ has been published in _Physics of the Earth and Planetary Interiors_. In this work, done in collaboration with [{{ site.data.authors.FP.name }}]({{ site.data.authors.FP.url }}) and [{{ site.data.authors.CG.name }}]({{ site.data.authors.CG.url }}), we study the process of magnetic reversals in the geodynamo. By imposing a stably-stratified layer below the outer core, and heterogeneous boundary conditions at the outer core, we observed that different geomagnetic solutions can be recovered, such as hemispheric dynamos and polarity reversals. We interpret these results using a low-dimensional model, and provide an interpretation for the stably-stratified layer as an electrically conducting boundary condition.
