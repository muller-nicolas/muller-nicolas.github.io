Our paper _Pressure and size dependence of roton emission and vortex creation by moving objects in He II in T → 0 limit: Generalized nonlocal Gross-Pitaevskii model_ has been published in _Physical Review Fluids_! In this work, done in collaboration with [{{ site.data.authors.LS.name }}]({{ site.data.authors.LS.url }}), [{{ site.data.authors.YS.name }}]({{ site.data.authors.YS.url }}), and [{{ site.data.authors.GK.name }}]({{ site.data.authors.GK.url }}), 
we use a generalized nonlocal Gross-Pitaevskii model whose interaction potential is chosen to reproduce the experimental dispersion relation of superfluid $^4$He from 0 to 24 bar. We use this model to study the critical velocity for vortex nucleation and roton emission at the wake of a moving obstacle of different sizes. We find that the critical velocity for roton emission decreases with pressure (critical Mach number from $0.247$ to $0.128$), while the critical velocity for vortex nucleation increases. For small objects such as negative ion bubbles, the gap between the two grows with pressure, suggesting that rotons could be more easily detected (in the absence of vortices) at high pressure. For large obstacles ($D \geq 1000\xi$), all curves collapse onto the vortex nucleation threshold of the standard GPE.
