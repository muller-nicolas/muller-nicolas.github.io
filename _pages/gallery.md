Evolution of a sphere moving at a constant speed in a superfluid that supports rotons. As the system evolves, rotons are emitted (in the form of density fluctuations), and also quantum vortex rings are nucleated at the wave of the obstacle (blue circles). # Geodynamo

I studied the emergence of polarity reversals and hemispheric dynamos in a geodynamo model with a stably-stratified layer below the outer core. You can find the article [here](/files/papers/Müller et al. - 2026 - Magnetic reversals in a geodynamo model with a stably–stratified layer.pdf).

### Polarity reversals

Polarity reversals in a geodynamo model with a stably-stratified layer below the outer core. An heterogeneous heat flux is imposed at the outer core with a pattern $$Y_3^0$$.
