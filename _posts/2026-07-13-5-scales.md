I participated in the [SCALES Training School](https://superfluid.troja.mff.cuni.cz/scales-school/) on *Turbulence and Vortex Dynamics* from 6-10 of July in Prague, where I shared my most recent results on the study [vortex nucleation and roton emission in superfluid $^4$He at different pressures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24075). Very happy to meet my colleagues and collaborators once again.
